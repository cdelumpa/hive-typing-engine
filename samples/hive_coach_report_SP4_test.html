1A · Counter-Type Considerations
Why This Matters
  • SP 4 Tenacity is one of the most commonly misread subtypes in the system — it presents as driven, resilient, and quietly self-sufficient in ways that can look like Type 1's integrity, Type 3's productivity, or simply 'not a Four.' If you lead with the standard Four description, you may lose this client early.
  • The counter-type's defining move is internalization: where SO or SX Fours externalize longing through expression or intensity, SP Fours tend to metabolize it privately — enduring, persisting, refusing to be defeated. The client's own language — 'envy' carried as something to work with rather than display — confirms this dynamic.
  • Naming the counter-type explicitly and early gives the client permission to say 'I don't fully see myself in the standard description' without taking that as disconfirming evidence. It actually deepens trust in the process.
  • The core wound (deficiency, longing, envy) is the same across all Four subtypes — only the strategy for managing it differs. For SP 4, the strategy is endurance and self-improvement rather than dramatization or seeking mirroring from others.
Standard vs. Counter-Type Presentation
  • Standard Four presentation: emotionally expressive, aesthetically attuned, comfortable with melancholy, pulls others toward their inner world, may openly dramatize longing or loss. Often visibly 'different.'
  • SP 4 Tenacity presentation: driven, disciplined, quietly resilient, reluctant to display vulnerability, tends to work through the feeling of deficiency rather than voice it. May appear more Type 1 or Type 3 to outside observers.
  • What they share: the core experience of 'something essential is missing in me,' the orientation toward authenticity and depth as highest values, the characteristic movement of envy-comparison, and the identity built around emotional depth even when that depth is carried privately.
  • The distinguishing motivation: the Tenacity Four is not performing resilience — they are genuinely refusing to be defeated by their own wound. The endurance is real. But underneath it, the longing and sense of deficiency are as structurally present as in any other Four subtype.
