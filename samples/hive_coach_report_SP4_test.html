Standard vs. Counter-Type Presentation
  • Standard Four: emotionally expressive, visibly moody, may romanticize suffering, seeks to be witnessed and understood, can appear dramatic or self-pitying. The longing is often on the surface.
  • SP Four (Tenacity): emotionally private, stoic, resilient, refuses to be defeated by the inner wound. The longing is equally present but carried internally rather than expressed. May look more like a driven, quietly disciplined person than a 'typical' Four.
