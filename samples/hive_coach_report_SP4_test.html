  • Envy is the Four's organizing emotional habit — not jealousy of specific possessions, but a longing for a quality of being, wholeness, or completeness that seems present in others and absent in the self. It is experienced as a gravitational pull away from the present moment toward an idealized elsewhere.
  • Fours tend to idealize what is absent and find what is present inadequate — a pattern that shows up in relationships, work, and self-perception. The grass is not greener elsewhere; it is simply that the present moment rarely matches the inner image of what it could be.
  • At their best, Fours bring genuine emotional depth, authenticity, and the capacity to hold complexity and difficulty without flinching — they do not smooth over what is real, and this is one of their most significant gifts.
