1A · Counter-Type Considerations
Why This Matters
  • SP 4 — Tenacity is the counter-type for Four, meaning it runs counter to the cultural stereotype of the type. Where the standard Four might be visibly expressive, romantic, or emotionally demonstrative, the SP 4 tends to be inward, self-sufficient, and quietly driven — more focused on enduring the inner sense of lack than on expressing it outwardly.
  • If you present the standard Four description without counter-type framing, the client may push back — not because the type is wrong, but because the description doesn't match their experience. That pushback would be diagnostically accurate and should not be read as type rejection.
  • The envy pattern in SP 4 tends to be internalized — less about overt comparison and more about a persistent, private sense of being behind, of others having something essential that they lack. The client's description ('pulls me away from what I actually have') is very SP 4 in its self-contained quality.
  • Counter-type Fours often have strong work ethics, appear competent and self-reliant, and may be mistaken for Ones or Threes. The counter-type frame also explains why 'tenacity' and 'emotional depth' co-exist as descriptors — the SP 4 endures the feeling rather than performing it.
Standard vs. Counter-Type Presentation
  • Standard SX or SO Fours: tend to be more visibly expressive, romantic, relationally intense, or focused on making an impression through emotional authenticity. The longing tends to have an outward orientation — toward a person, an experience, an audience.
  • SP 4 Tenacity: the longing is more private and self-directed — oriented toward what's missing inside rather than what's missing out there. The survival strategy is to build inner resilience and not be defeated by the deficit. This can look like drive, discipline, or quiet stoicism from the outside.
  • Both share the same core motivation: the sense that something essential is missing, the commitment to authenticity, and the Four's characteristic attunement to what is beautiful, real, and meaningful. The difference is in the direction the longing faces and the strategy used to manage it.
  • Distinguishing motivation: the SP 4 is not trying to be seen as unique or to have their depth recognized — they are trying to survive the inner experience of lack without falling apart. Tenacity is the operative word.
