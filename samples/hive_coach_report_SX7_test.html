The Core Pattern
  • The Type 7 worldview: the world is full of stimulating possibility, and pain — boredom, limitation, loss, grief — is something to be outrun, reframed, or transmuted into something more interesting. This isn't laziness or immaturity; it's a deep structural orientation to experience.
  • The 7's core fear is being trapped in pain or deprivation — and the mental habit of planning, reframing, and jumping to the next exciting thing is the ego's solution to that fear. The problem is that the solution bypasses what needs to be felt.
  • The core motivation is freedom: freedom of experience, freedom of mind, freedom from limitation. When that freedom is threatened — by commitment, by loss, by difficulty — the 7's characteristic move is to find an exit, mental or physical.
  • The gift of this type, when the pattern is working well: genuine generosity of spirit, the ability to hold multiple possibilities simultaneously, and a natural talent for activating possibility in others — which this client named directly as their greatest strength.

Wings — Type 6 and Type 8
  • Type 7's wings are Type 6 (the Loyalist) and Type 8 (the Challenger). These are meaningfully different flavors of Seven.
  • A 7 with a strong 6 wing (7w6) tends to be warmer, more relational and loyal, more aware of others' needs, and more likely to feel genuine anxiety beneath the enthusiasm. The scatter is softer; the commitment to specific people runs deeper.
  • A 7 with a strong 8 wing (7w8) tends to be more assertive, more driven, more willing to push through obstacles, and more comfortable with confrontation. The energy is bigger; the shadow can be more excessive or controlling.
  • Let the client lead here — ask which wing sounds more familiar and why. Don't force it. Some clients live right in the middle.
Try asking: 'There are two flavors of Seven that might feel familiar — one that's warmer and more loyal, more aware of the people around them; one that's more driven and assertive, more willing to push. Which feels closer to how you tend to show up?'
