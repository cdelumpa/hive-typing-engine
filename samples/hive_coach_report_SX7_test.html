The Core Pattern

  • Sevens are Head-center types, but their relationship to fear is different from the 6 (who scans for threat) and the 5 (who withdraws to conserve). The 7 moves preemptively: rather than sitting with anxiety, they convert it into planning, anticipation, and activity. The world is always on the verge of being either fascinating or limiting — and the job is to stay on the right side of that line.
