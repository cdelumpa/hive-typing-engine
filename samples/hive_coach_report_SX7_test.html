The Core Pattern
  • The Type 7 worldview: the world is full of stimulating, nourishing experiences — and the task is to stay in them and away from what feels painful, limiting, or like a trap. This isn't conscious strategy; it's a structural orientation that feels like enthusiasm from the inside and like avoidance from the outside.
  • The core fear is deprivation — being stuck in pain, boredom, or limitation with no way out. The response is a perpetual orientation toward what's next, what's possible, what's interesting. Options are oxygen.
  • Reframing is the 7's central cognitive move: transform the painful or difficult into something positive, find the silver lining, project forward to where things will be better. This is a genuine gift and a genuine defense — often simultaneously.
  • The 7's deep longing is for satisfaction — real, lasting, fully arrived-at contentment. The irony is that the constant movement toward the next thing tends to keep real satisfaction just out of reach.
