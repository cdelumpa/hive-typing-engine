Navigating the Enneagram System

Your Stress and Security Points

The Enneagram system shows you where you tend to move when you're feeling secure and in flow, and where you tend to move under an unusual amount of stress. Both moves happen in the background, part of the same internal operating system as your Home Base type and your Wings. When you access these points intentionally they become valuable resources to expand your range of choices in any situation.
6
Stress Point · Type 6
The Questioner
When pressure builds past what steady acceptance can absorb, Nines move toward Type 6. You may show either the strengths or the challenges of the Questioner, depending on the circumstances.
The easy calm gives way to anxiety, doubt, and worst-case thinking.
Noticed early, the movement signals that something important has been pushed down.
The same line carries you toward Six when you are mobilizing for action, not only when strained.
Accessing the High Side of Type 6
Useful vigilance, loyalty, and the commitment to face what matters rather than smooth it over.
3
Security Point · Type 3
The Performer
When Nines feel genuinely safe and supported, they move toward Type 3. Here too you may show either the strengths or the challenges of the Performer, depending on the circumstances.
The diffusion gives way to direction, focus, and energy.
Priorities come into focus and get pursued, without waiting for someone else to set the agenda.
The movement can also arrive paradoxically, when you are overwhelmed or exhausted rather than settled.
Accessing the High Side of Type 3
Stepping into your own goals rather than deferring. Letting yourself want something and moving toward it.

Putting Your Resources to Work

These movements are easier to work with once you can see them happening. The skill is catching yourself in the moment, and it builds with practice.
Catch the shift
Notice the moment the easy calm turns to worry, or the diffusion turns to drive. Early is better than accurate.
Notice the impact
Ask what the people around you are experiencing while you are in that energy. The shift lands on them too.
Then choose
Seen clearly, the movement becomes something you use on purpose rather than something that happens to you.
