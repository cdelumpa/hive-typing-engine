InsightOut Enneagram Report Anders Wennerstrom · Type 9 — The Peacemaker
Exploring Your Type Hypothesis (continued)
The Type 9 Peacemaker patterns described on the previous page usually run in the background, like an "internal operating system". The real power of the Enneagram is learning how to expand beyond our default patterns. The first step is seeing how our patterns show up in the real world. Here are some things to look for.
At Your Best
Steady warmth. A receptive presence that makes people feel genuinely welcome.
Holding multiple perspectives. Non-judgmental and inclusive, making you a trusted mediator.
Steady presence. A calm, stabilizing force that becomes real strength when embodied.
Your Growing Edge
Self-forgetting. You may manage everyone else's comfort while quietly losing track of your own.
Merging. Agreeableness, repeated over time, can make you forget your own agenda.
Quiet anger. Unacknowledged, it turns into inertia or passive resistance.

Communication, Conflict, and Decision-Making Styles

Communication Style
Inclusive and Indirect
You listen more than you assert. People rarely leave knowing what you actually think.
Your real position forms later, in private. In the moment, you may not know it.
Maybe, perhaps, I don't mind. Qualifiers take the edges off your point.
Conflict Style
Go Along to Get Along
You find the reading where everyone is fine. What you reframe returns larger.
Discomfort gets smoothed, not named. Relief now, a vague heaviness later.
When pushed, you may dig in, become stubborn, and slow down the pace.
Decision-Making Style
Every Option Counts
Every choice has a case, so deciding feels like discarding rather than gaining.
Your own preference surfaces last. Choosing on the spot is hard.
Knowing what you don't want comes first. That is a usable starting point.

Catching Your Patterns in the Moment

Signs the Pattern Is Showing Up
You say "yes" when you want to say "no".
You withhold your opinion to not rock the boat.
You ignore the tension in your body.
Interrupting the Pattern
Make a counteroffer instead of a flat-out "no".
Speak your truth with no attachments.
Pause, breathe, and be curious about the tension.
