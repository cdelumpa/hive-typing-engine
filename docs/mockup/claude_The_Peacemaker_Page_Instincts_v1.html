Instincts & Subtypes

Alongside your home base type, you have a dominant instinct, one of three innate drives that shape where you put your energy. We use all three instincts but one tends to dominate. Your subtype combines your core type with your dominant instinct, creating a distinct flavor of the Peacemaker pattern.

The Three Instincts

SPSelf-Preservation
Focused On
Safety, comfort, health, and the practical resources of daily life: securing what you need physically and materially.
SOSocial
Focused On
Belonging, group dynamics, and your place within a community: alliances, fairness, collective causes, knowing where you stand.
SXOne-to-One
Focused On
Deep, intense connection with specific people or experiences: chemistry, magnetism, and the quality of individual bonds.
