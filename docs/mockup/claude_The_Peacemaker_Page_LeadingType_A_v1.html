Type 9 · The Peacemaker

Based on your responses to the questionnaire, we're presenting you with Type 9 - The Peacemaker as the strongest hypothesis of your home base Enneagram type. We encourage you to hold this as a hypothesis until you've read through this report and/or have discussed it with your coach. Remember, you are the ultimate authority on your Enneagram type.
9
Core Motivation
To maintain inner and outer peace, stay connected to others, and avoid the conflict and discomfort that come with asserting your own priorities.
Worldview
The world has an underlying unity that conflict threatens. Your role is to maintain harmony and find common ground.
Core Belief
Keeping the peace keeps you connected. Asserting yourself risks the harmony you depend on.
In Your Own Words
"I project a calm presence and this helps others regulate and calm themselves as well. It can be a powerful antidote to fear, anxiety, and other emotions that disrupt connection… I overthink things at times, perhaps often. This slows me down and leads to indecision."
The Peacemaker at a Glance
What Nines Want
To have inner and outer peace, and to experience a sense of belonging
Where Nines Turn Their Attention
The environment around them, potential conflict, and other people's agendas and claims on them
What Nines Tend to Avoid
Conflict, discomfort, and feeling disregarded or disconnected
Driving Emotion
Anger. Though present, anger goes unacknowledged in order to preserve inner and outer peace.

Typical Patterns of the Type 9 Peacemaker

1Thinking
As a Peacemaker, your attention goes outward to everyone else's positions, agendas, and comfort, often more readily than to your own. Locating your own stance is the harder task.
2Feeling
As a Peacemaker, you carry a steady, even temperament, but that calm can sit on top of feelings smoothed over rather than fully felt. Anger, in particular, goes quiet first.
3Behaving
As a Peacemaker, your behavior organizes around not making waves: going along, smoothing things over, keeping everyone comfortable. Your steadiness is a gift, and sometimes a hiding place.
